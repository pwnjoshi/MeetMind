## Description (max 4000)

```
Manent records your meetings and writes the minutes for you, entirely on your iPhone.

There is no account to create and no server that receives your audio. Speech recognition, speaker separation and minutes writing all run on the device. Once the models are downloaded, Manent works in airplane mode.

WHAT YOU GET
- Record a meeting with live captions while you talk.
- Minutes with a summary, decisions, open issues and action items with owners and deadlines.
- Speaker separation, so the transcript shows who said what. Name a voice once and Manent can recognise it in later meetings.
- Hindi, English and mixed Hindi-English (Hinglish) meetings. Show Hindi in Devanagari or in Roman letters.
- Vidur, an assistant that answers questions about your own meetings ("What did we decide about the budget?") and links each answer to the minutes or the moment in the recording.
- Import an existing audio file and get minutes from it.
- Export minutes as a PDF with your letterhead and signature, or share them by mail.
- Send action items to Apple Reminders and add follow-ups to Apple Calendar when you choose to.
- Attach photos of whiteboards and slides to a meeting.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Recordings, transcripts and minutes stay on this iPhone.
- No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- The only network use is a one-time download of the open AI models you choose, from Hugging Face. No meeting content is sent.
- Delete All Data in Settings removes everything Manent stored.

HOW IT WORKS
Manent uses open models that run on your iPhone: Whisper for speech recognition, a speaker-separation model, and Qwen3 1.7B for writing minutes and answering Vidur questions. Their licences are listed in Settings, About and Licences. The models are large downloads (about 0.5 GB to 1.3 GB each, see Settings, Offline Analysis Models), so connect to Wi-Fi the first time. Analysis of a long meeting takes minutes and uses battery, and it is slower on older iPhones. Minutes are drafts: review them before you share them, because a small on-device model can make mistakes with names, numbers and dates.

Manent needs microphone access to record, and speech recognition access for live captions. Calendar, Reminders, camera and photo access are optional and requested only when you use those features. Calendar access only writes follow-up events you request. Reminders access exports selected Manent action items and updates those exported reminders from Manent.

You are responsible for telling participants that you are recording and obtaining consent where required by local law.

Questions? See the support page linked on this listing.
```
